# Code of Conduct

Better YGO contributors are expected to keep discussion technical, respectful,
and focused on improving the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not accepted.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
these rules are violated.

Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner through the contact
information on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed confidentially
and handled according to their context and severity.
